When does the solver open a warehouse?

It does not take a warehouse count as input. It opens the next candidate that passes every rule below, then stops when none do. Cap is 28.

  1. Phase 1: candidate county is Austin, Houston, San Antonio, or DFW. Phase 2: any county on a mapped corridor.
  2. County sits within 40 miles of a mapped interstate / US corridor.
  3. Road distance to that county ≤ max road miles (default 80).
  4. At least 70 road miles from every warehouse already open.
  5. Leased lot ≥ 40,000 sq ft, or a Census town pin if the county has no such lot.
  6. Territory can staff 1 technician per 5,000 residents and 1 engineer per 40,000.
  7. Uncovered finished homes in range ≥ 12,000 after closer warehouses take what their crew can carry.
  8. Warehouse capacity = technicians × homes one warehouse can carry. Lower that number and each open warehouse covers fewer homes, so more warehouses open.
  9. If a yard is in outage, a replacement must sit within ~90 miles of that yard. West Texas cannot replace Jacintoport.

Bastrop and Cleveland are town pins. They are not leases. Phase 2 + leftover demand ≥ 12,000 homes with 70-mile separation from Houston/Austin yards is enough for the greedy loop to drop a pin there. That is not a commissioned neighborhood.

Machine form: policy/warehouse.rego (Open Policy Agent).

opa eval -d policy/warehouse.rego -i candidate.json "data.base.warehouse.allow"
